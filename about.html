About Me

Tomo Reynolds is a jazz pianist based in Toronto, studying in the Bachelor of Music program at Humber Polytechnic. He has performed at venues including the Rex Hotel Jazz & Blues Bar.

Available for jazz engagements, private events, and corporate functions, both as a solo pianist and in ensemble settings. Private lessons are also available.
